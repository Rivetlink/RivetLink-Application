Direct connections
- Outside your own network, RivetLink now tries to connect the two computers directly instead of sending everything through your server. This gives lower latency and less server traffic. If a direct connection is not possible, it falls back to the relay by itself.
- The bottom of the viewer shows how you are connected: Local network, Direct or Via relay.
- Your server needs STUN for this (see the RivetLink-Docker README, "Direct connections"). Without it, everything works as before.

New Help page
- A Help page in the sidebar explains how to connect on your own network, add a server, put a computer on your relay, and what to do when something does not work, with buttons to the right screens.
