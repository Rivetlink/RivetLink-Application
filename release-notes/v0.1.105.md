Remote access through your own relay
- Reach your Linux Home Node from outside your network, including its login screen. Connect finds the host at your relay and opens an end-to-end encrypted tunnel. The relay only forwards ciphertext.
- Run your own relay on a small Ubuntu VPS: https://github.com/Rivetlink/RivetLink-Docker (relay image 0.1.105 or newer).
- Turn it on at the Home Node under Settings → Ubuntu physical console → Via relay.

Post-quantum encryption
- Direct connections now use a hybrid X25519 + ML-KEM-768 handshake, which protects against "harvest now, decrypt later".
- Once a host has used it, RivetLink refuses a weaker connection to that host.

Update the Home Node and the device you connect from. Older hosts keep working on the local network.
