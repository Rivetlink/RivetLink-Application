A new look for RivetLink
- Calmer dark design with one accent color and a fixed sidebar: Devices, Receive help and Settings.
- Devices: one list of cards instead of tabs. Each card shows whether the computer is online and whether RivetLink reaches it over the local network or through your relay.
- Receive help: a large code with three short steps, and a clear card when a helper is connected.
- Settings: grouped into General, Security, Servers, Ubuntu console and About & updates. Servers and resource usage moved in here.
- Viewer: the toolbar at the bottom is restyled, with the screen picker in the toolbar, a connection-quality dot and a labelled Disconnect button.
- The update dialog now shows what is new in each release.

Nothing changed in how connections work.
